## How to interpret this list

- **Official domain** is the domain recorded in the manifest; it may differ from the final redirect or third-party ATS domain used to serve job listings.
- **Careers page** is the recorded official careers URL, not a guarantee that it is currently reachable.
- **Scraper module** identifies the corresponding Python scraper under `backend/app/scrapers/`.
- A scraper is only considered live-test passing when its test run returns valid, non-empty records with `source_job_id`, `title`, and `url`. A domain appearing here is not proof of a passing test.
